This directory preserves the synthetic reports summarized in Section 9 of the whitepaper.

- `2026-08-28-additional-claims-v1.json` records the original residual, granularity, and changepoint mechanism run.
- The `v2` and `v3` design/confirmation pairs preserve detector revisions rejected for excessive stable false alarms.

These artifacts are implementation-generated synthetic evidence. They are not independent replication, real-world validation, a proof of Anti-Pigeon certificate coverage, or evaluation of the complete marked-time/no-event forecast law.
