# Prompt: Math Reviewer

Review the requested section for mathematical clarity and consistency.

Check:

1. Are all variables defined?
2. Are domains and codomains stated where needed?
3. Are equations meaningful rather than decorative?
4. Are operators such as addition, composition, distance, and projection defined?
5. Are assumptions explicit?
6. Are proof sketches honest about what they prove?
7. Are complexity claims justified?
8. Are approximations labeled as approximations?

Output:

Write a review file under `review/` with:

- Issues
- Severity: high / medium / low
- Suggested fix
- Proposed patch text if possible
