## Bugs Found During Implementation

1. P2: positive underflow could produce a zero TV increment. Outward arithmetic
   now treats computed zero conservatively; explicit exact-zero cases are separate.
   The former 2/9 counterexample and safe-discard underflow have rational regressions.
2. P2: product-before-ratio subnormal arithmetic understated a normal planning
   count (512 vs 604). Ratio-first evaluation and numeric-domain rejection repair
   it; the 137-stream pilot calculation is unchanged.
3. P2: raw spans from masked regex captures could restore quoted bytes as direct
   fields. Captures crossing masked bytes now abstain on turn/query/text paths.
4. P2, inherited: capitalized pronouns could be treated as named actors before
   fallback. Turn and query actor extraction now reject pronouns, including tested
   contractions; identity-aware resolution retains its separate authority rules.

The old multiline-quote test expectation was stale: it required the now-prohibited
direct mechanism extraction. Its replacement strengthens raw/summary retention,
field abstention and inactive quoted-reference assertions. No failed scientific
criterion was weakened or relabeled to obtain a pass.

production-evaluation and storage tests pass; focused race checks and vet pass.

arithmetic, status/claim and timing consistency tests cover the correction.

and OpenClaw installations remain untouched. No commit or push is part of this patch.

valid. A current-state envelope cannot certify historical-query weights. Standard

tightness, historical-path fidelity and loaded serving tails remain separate work.
